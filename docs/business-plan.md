## 1. Executive summary

The business is a design-led creative studio serving individuals, small businesses, growing brands and established organisations. It provides brand identity, digital design, advertising and campaign creatives, product visuals and creative direction.

The business will compete through thoughtful creative work, clear communication, reliable delivery and accessible entry-level services—not by being the cheapest designer. Pricing is quote-led: public visitors can understand the services, while each project receives a scope-specific quotation based on deliverables, complexity, timeline and usage requirements.

The first operating phase is intentionally lean. Use the existing website, WhatsApp and social channels to acquire clients; manage enquiries, quotations, project stages and payments through the existing admin workspace; and reinvest revenue into better portfolio work, brand assets and business systems.

## 4. Services

The five service departments are:

1. **Brand Identity:** logo design, visual identity, colour and typography systems, basic guidelines and selected brand assets.
2. **Digital Design:** social media graphics, content sets, campaign creatives and digital announcements.
3. **Advertising & Campaigns:** business advertisements, promotional flyers, event graphics and campaign concepts.
4. **Product Visuals:** product advertisements, product displays, packaging concepts and business mockups.
5. **Creative Direction:** visual concept development, art direction, brand presentations and custom creative solutions.

A logo is not automatically a complete brand identity. Each quotation must state exactly which assets and formats are included.

## 5. Pricing approach

### Commercial rule

**Important:** These are proposed launch prices, not verified market benchmarks or permanent fixed fees. Review each price against design time, complexity, software/data costs, revision load and desired margin before accepting a project. The admin catalogue allows prices to be changed without editing the website.

### Quote calculation
- Add each deliverable as a separate line item.
- Quantity × unit price = line total.
- Subtotal − approved discount = project total.
- Initial payment defaults to 50%, but may be changed per project.
- Outstanding balance is calculated from verified payments, not pending payment screenshots or claims.

Avoid discounting a package and then charging full individual rates for the same included items. Bundle pricing must be intentional.

## 6. Packages and recurring work

These are sales structures, not rigid public price lists:

- **Essential:** one focused design project with a clearly defined deliverable.
- **Growth:** several coordinated designs for a growing business or campaign.
- **Partner:** ongoing monthly creative support with an agreed scope, response time and deliverable allowance.

A retainer should only be offered when workload, availability, payment dates and monthly deliverables can be reliably managed.

## 7. Client journey and operating workflow

1. **Enquiry:** client submits the website form or contacts the studio.
2. **Brief review:** clarify the audience, objective, content, reference materials, deliverables and deadline.
3. **Quotation:** create an itemised quote with scope, price, revisions, timeline, validity and terms.
4. **Approval:** record the client's acceptance; do not treat a sent quote as accepted.
5. **Initial payment:** verify the actual transaction before production starts.
6. **Production:** work to the approved brief and record meaningful project-stage updates.
7. **Review:** send previews and collect consolidated feedback within the included revision allowance.
8. **Final payment:** verify the remaining amount.
9. **Delivery:** provide the agreed files and formats.
10. **Close-out:** request feedback and ask permission before using the work in the public portfolio.

## 8. Default business policies

- Default initial payment: 50% before production.
- Default included revisions: two reasonable rounds, unless the quotation says otherwise.
- A change to the approved brief or additional deliverables is new scope and may require a revised quote.
- Timelines begin after the brief, required assets and initial payment are received.
- Rush requests depend on availability and may attract an additional fee.
- Final files are delivered after agreed payment conditions are fulfilled.
- Never claim a payment is verified based only on a screenshot.
- Record payment method, reference and date; do not store card numbers or banking passwords.
- Obtain permission or agree portfolio-use terms before publishing client work.
- Explain cancellation, refund and rescheduling terms before accepting payment, consistent with applicable law.

## 9. Marketing and client acquisition

### Primary channels
- Website portfolio and enquiry form.
- WhatsApp Business for direct enquiries and follow-up.
- Facebook and Instagram for visual work, process and case studies.
- Referrals from satisfied clients and complementary service providers.
- Direct, personalised outreach to businesses whose presentation can be improved.

### Content themes
- Before/after design breakdowns.
- Short case studies explaining the brief and creative decisions.
- Product and campaign mockups.
- Practical branding and social media advice.
- Behind-the-scenes process and work-in-progress previews.
- Client feedback, only with permission.

Show real work. Do not invent client names, testimonials, campaign results or performance metrics. Label concept work clearly as a concept.

### Sales principles
- Lead with the client's business problem, not a list of software or design effects.
- Ask for the brief before committing to a fixed fee.
- Respond clearly and professionally.
- Follow up politely after sending a quotation.
- Do not promise results that the design alone cannot guarantee.

## 10. Delivery and quality control

Before sending a final file, check:
- All supplied text is accurate and complete.
- Names, dates, phone numbers, prices and addresses are correct.
- Layout hierarchy and readability work at phone size.
- Colours and typography match the approved direction.
- Spelling and alignment have been checked.
- Export dimensions and file format match the quotation.
- Final files are named clearly and stored safely.

## 11. Business systems

The existing website and admin workspace provide:
- Public portfolio, case studies, enquiry form and reviews.
- Editable service catalogue.
- Itemised quotations and browser print-to-PDF.
- WhatsApp and email handoff links.
- Quotation statuses: Draft, Sent, Accepted, Declined, Expired and Cancelled.
- Payment ledger with Pending, Verified and Rejected states.
- Outstanding balance calculated from verified payments.
- Enquiry and project tracking.
- Portfolio and studio-profile administration.

Payments are manually recorded. No payment gateway is connected. A future client portal should only be built after a secure client identity and record-ownership model is designed and tested.

## 12. Key performance indicators

Review monthly:
- Number of qualified enquiries.
- Enquiry-to-quotation conversion rate.
- Quotation acceptance rate.
- Average project value.
- Revenue collected versus outstanding balances.
- Average delivery time.
- Average revision rounds per project.
- Repeat-client and referral share.
- Profit per project after time and operating costs.

Do not judge success only by the number of enquiries. Track profitable completed work and repeat relationships.

## 13. First 90-day operating roadmap

- Reach out to relevant local businesses with specific, respectful proposals.
- Collect feedback from completed clients.

### Days 61–90: Improve and grow
- Identify the most profitable and most requested services.
- Refine bundles and consider a small retainer offer.
- Publish stronger case studies.
- Improve delivery checklists and turnaround estimates.
- Decide whether a client portal or payment gateway is justified by actual workload.

## 14. Risks and controls

| Risk | Control |
|---|---|
| Scope creep | Itemised scope, revision limits and change quotes |
| Late or disputed payments | Initial payment, manual verification and payment ledger |
| Underpricing | Monthly cost/time review and editable starting prices |
| Inconsistent quality | Pre-delivery quality checklist |
| Weak portfolio | Build real case studies and request permission |
| Name conflict | Formal CAC and trademark clearance before rebrand |
| Overcommitting | Agree timelines after reviewing workload |
| Client data exposure | Admin-only RLS and a separately designed client portal |
