# AQREVO — Internal Business & Operating Plan

**Document status:** Working plan, November 2026  
**Working brand name:** AQREVO — *The Creative House*  
**Descriptor:** The Creative House  
**Tagline:** Make Ideas Iconic.  
**Brand status:** Provisional. Do not rename the public Envision Studio website, buy a domain, file a trademark or print permanent brand materials until name clearance and owner approval are complete.

## 1. Business direction

AQREVO is planned as an independent creative house helping individuals, small businesses, growing brands and established organisations communicate through thoughtful visual design.

The business will compete through clarity, originality, reliable execution and useful creative thinking—not by being the cheapest designer available.

**Mission**  
Turn ideas into meaningful visual experiences through thoughtful design, creative strategy and professional execution.

**Vision**  
Build a recognised creative brand known for originality, quality and helping businesses become visually distinctive.

**Brand promise**  
Thoughtful creativity, clear communication, agreed scope and attention to detail.

**Personality**  
Original, confident, intelligent, refined and innovative.

## 2. Target customers

### Individuals and early-stage businesses
Typical needs include event graphics, birthday designs, simple promotional flyers, first logos, social media graphics and starter brand identity.

What matters to this segment:
- Clear, accessible entry-level pricing.
- A simple enquiry and quotation process.
- Help understanding what design they actually need.
- Predictable deliverables and timelines.

### Growing and established businesses
Typical needs include brand refreshes, campaign visuals, product advertising, coordinated social media content, mockups and ongoing creative support.

What matters to this segment:
- A clear creative brief and project scope.
- Consistency across multiple assets.
- Professional presentation and dependable delivery.
- Custom quotations for complex or recurring work.

The same studio can serve both segments by keeping entry-level services accessible while quoting larger work according to its real scope, complexity, time and responsibility.

## 3. Service departments

1. **Brand Identity** — logo design, basic identity systems, brand refreshes, colour and typography direction, brand guidelines and selected brand assets.
2. **Digital Design** — social media graphics, coordinated content sets and digital campaign visuals.
3. **Advertising & Campaigns** — promotional flyers, event designs, business advertisements and campaign concepts.
4. **Product Visuals** — product advertisements, product mockups, business mockups and presentation visuals.
5. **Creative Direction** — custom concepts, art direction and larger creative engagements quoted from a defined brief.

### Service boundaries

- A logo is not automatically a complete brand identity.
- A mockup is a presentation visual, not physical product manufacturing or printing.
- A social media bundle has a defined maximum number of designs; additional formats or designs are quoted separately.
- Creative direction is custom-quoted.
- Printing, photography, copywriting, paid advertising, source files and third-party licences are excluded unless specifically listed in the quotation.

## 5. Quotation and payment policy

### Standard quotation contents
Every quotation should include:
- Unique quotation number.
- Client and business details.
- Project title and brief.
- Itemised services, quantities and unit prices.
- Included deliverables and exclusions.
- Subtotal, approved discount and total.
- Initial payment and remaining balance.
- Delivery estimate and quotation validity.
- Included revision rounds.
- Terms and conditions.

### Default terms
- A 50% initial payment is the default before production begins, unless a different arrangement is agreed in writing.
- Two reasonable revision rounds are included by default.
- Revisions must stay within the approved brief. New concepts, new deliverables or major changes to supplied content are additional scope.
- Additional work is quoted and approved before it begins.
- Delivery dates are agreed after the brief and assets are complete.
- Final files are delivered after the agreed payment terms are fulfilled.
- Rush work is subject to capacity and may carry an additional fee.
- Client portfolio use is subject to permission or agreed terms.

These are operating defaults, not a substitute for legal review. Refund, cancellation, privacy and consumer-facing terms should be reviewed before launch.

### Payment recording
- Record a payment only after the client says it has been made.
- New payment entries remain **Pending** until the transaction is checked against the actual receiving account.
- A screenshot or transfer notification alone is not final proof of receipt.
- Only verified payments reduce the outstanding balance.
- Never store card numbers, PINs, passwords or banking login details.

## 6. Client and project workflow

1. **Enquiry received** — capture contact details, service requested, brief, budget and target date.
2. **Brief review** — confirm goals, content, supplied assets, scope and missing information.
3. **Quotation prepared** — build an itemised quote with timeline, revisions, payment terms and validity.
4. **Quotation sent** — mark it as sent only after it has actually been delivered to the client.
5. **Acceptance recorded** — record acceptance only after the client confirms.
6. **Initial payment verified** — production starts once the agreed payment is confirmed.
7. **Production** — track the project stage and internal notes.
8. **Client review** — collect consolidated feedback and manage included revisions.
9. **Final payment and delivery** — verify the balance and deliver the agreed files.
10. **Closeout** — archive the project, request feedback and seek permission before adding it to the public portfolio.

The admin system separates enquiries, quotations, quotation line items, payments and project updates. This avoids treating one enquiry as if it were the entire client and project history.

## 7. Website and technology

The current public website remains **Envision Studio** until the AQREVO name is cleared and the rebrand is approved.

The business operations workspace is designed to support:
- Editable service catalogue and starting prices.
- Itemised quotations and printable quotation output.
- Client quotation status tracking.
- Payment records and verification.
- Project stages, due dates and internal notes.
- Portfolio projects, reviews and studio profile management.

Security principles:
- Business records remain admin-only under Supabase Row Level Security.
- Public visitors may read only active services intentionally marked public.
- Enquiries may be submitted publicly but must not be publicly readable.
- Never expose Supabase service-role credentials in the browser.
- Test admin access and RLS before collecting real customer information.

## 8. Marketing and customer acquisition

Priority channels:
- **Portfolio-led social content:** show finished work, design breakdowns, before/after comparisons and real project stories.
- **WhatsApp Business:** clear service descriptions, enquiry handling and quotation follow-up.
- **Facebook and Instagram:** business-specific design samples and educational content.
- **Referrals:** request reviews and referrals after successful delivery.
- **Case studies:** explain the brief, creative direction, deliverables and real outcome. Do not invent client results or performance metrics.

Content should demonstrate how design solves a communication problem, not merely display attractive graphics.

## 9. Financial discipline

Do not assume that a price is profitable because it is affordable to a client.

For each service, begin recording:
- Time spent on briefing, design, revisions and delivery.
- Software, data, stock assets, fonts and other direct costs.
- Number of revision rounds actually used.
- Rush work and communication overhead.
- Final amount received and any unpaid balance.

Review starting prices monthly. Raise, restructure or retire services when the actual time and costs show that the current price is unsustainable. Do not publish a revised price until it is approved.

A reliable profit forecast should only be prepared after real cost and delivery-time records exist.

## 11. Monthly management review

At each monthly review, assess:
- Enquiries by service and customer segment.
- Quotes sent, accepted, declined and expired.
- Revenue received versus verified outstanding balances.
- Average delivery time and revision rounds.
- Actual time/cost per service.
- Profitability and demand by service.
- Client feedback, repeat work and referrals.
- Any operational or privacy/security issues.

The monthly AQREVO pricing review automation should make recommendations only. It must not change live prices, quotations or policies without owner approval.
