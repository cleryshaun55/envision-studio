# AQREVO — Brand Identity Guide

**Version:** 1.0 working identity  
**Brand:** AQREVO  
**Descriptor:** The Creative House  
**Tagline:** Make Ideas Iconic.  
**Status:** Owner-approved creative direction. This is a working visual identity, not a trademark opinion or legal clearance.

## 1. Brand foundation

AQREVO is a creative house that turns business ideas into clear, memorable visual communication. The studio serves individuals, small businesses, growing brands and established organisations.

**Positioning:** Thoughtful creative design for brands that want to communicate clearly and look distinctive.

**Mission:** Transform ideas into meaningful visual experiences through thoughtful design, creative strategy and professional execution.

**Brand promise:** Clear thinking, intentional design, careful execution and transparent project communication.

**Personality:** Confident, original, intelligent, refined, approachable and resourceful.

**Tagline:** Make Ideas Iconic.

## 2. Logo system

The current working emblem is a compact geometric AQ monogram:
- The angular A structure signals direction, foundations and forward movement.
- The rounded Q gesture introduces continuity and a recognisable signature.
- The contrast between white and electric blue creates recognition on a deep navy tile.

The emblem is currently used with the AQREVO wordmark and the descriptor THE CREATIVE HOUSE. Treat this as the approved digital working mark. A final custom-drawn vector wordmark and professional trademark review remain future identity-production steps.

**Primary lockup:** Emblem + AQREVO wordmark + small descriptor.

**Emblem-only use:** Favicon, profile avatar, social watermark, app icon and compact signatures.

**Clear space:** Keep at least the width of the emblem's inner Q stroke clear around the mark.

**Minimum digital sizes:** Emblem 24 px; full lockup 140 px wide. Prefer larger sizes for print.

**Never:** stretch, skew, rotate, add bevels, use glow effects, change the letterform, put the mark over busy imagery, or rebuild it with ordinary typed letters.

**Ratio guidance:** Use ivory and white for most page space, navy for structure and important contrast, and electric blue as a deliberate accent. Blue should guide attention, not cover every element.

**Avoid:** full-page black backgrounds, excessive gradients, neon, metallic gold, glitter, random colour changes and low-contrast text.

## 4. Typography

Recommended system:
- **Headlines:** Montserrat, weight 700–800; use strong, short statements.
- **Body:** Poppins, weight 400–500; keep paragraphs readable and calm.
- **Labels and metadata:** a restrained monospace or compact sans-serif, uppercase with generous letter spacing.

Use no more than two main type families plus an optional utility face. Keep hierarchy obvious: one headline, one supporting statement, one action.

## 5. Layout and graphic language

- Use confident alignment, generous whitespace and clear grids.
- Pair large editorial headlines with carefully arranged examples of real design work.
- Use simple geometric lines, rounded rectangles and cropped frames sparingly.
- Make service cards easy to scan; never let decoration compete with the work.
- Use blue for links, selected states, small markers and key words.
- Use navy for primary buttons and dark anchor sections.

## 6. Photography and portfolio direction

Show the actual product, business, service or design outcome. Use bright, natural and purposeful lighting. Prefer authentic textures, realistic materials and business-specific environments.

Do not rely on generic plants, flowers, cinematic smoke, random 3D shapes or stock imagery that does not explain the client's product. Avoid plastic-looking AI faces and overprocessed mockups.

Portfolio entries must be real approved work or clearly labelled concept studies. Never imply a concept project was commissioned by a real client.

## 7. Voice and messaging

AQREVO should sound:
- Clear, capable and human.
- Confident without exaggerated promises.
- Creative without vague jargon.
- Friendly without being childish.
- Professional without sounding distant.

Prefer: "Let's shape the idea", "Designed with purpose", "A clear visual direction", "Make Ideas Iconic."

Avoid: "We are the best in the world", guaranteed sales claims, empty buzzwords and hard-sell pressure.

## 8. Applications

Apply the same logo, palette and typography to:
- Website and admin-facing quotation documents.
- Social media profile and cover artwork.
- Proposal and quotation templates.
- Portfolio presentations and case studies.
- Client onboarding and project updates.
- Watermarks and final design previews.

Keep the same core system while allowing each client project to have its own art direction.

## 9. Release checklist

Before permanent printing or legal filings:
1. Complete professional trademark clearance for AQREVO and similar marks in relevant service classes.
2. Confirm CAC business-name availability separately.
3. Confirm domain and social handle availability.
4. Commission or refine final vector master files and wordmark.
5. Test the emblem at favicon, avatar, print and monochrome sizes.
6. Confirm the new business email and replace old Envision Studio contact details.
7. Update all social profiles and client-facing documents consistently.

**Important:** Website rebranding is approved. Trademark registration, CAC registration, domain purchase and social handle changes are not represented as completed.
